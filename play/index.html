Name GameBASEBALL EDITION …

Name a big leaguer.

Take turns naming players who made the majors. The next person has to name a player whose first name starts with the first letter of your player's last name.

You play Ryne Sandberg → S to the next person, who might play Shawon Dunston. 15 seconds a turn, three strikes and you're out.

House rules
  • Must have played in at least one MLB game. Real name or the name they went by both count — Babe Ruth = George Herman Ruth.
  • First and last name required. "Ichiro" won't cut it — say Ichiro Suzuki.
  • Suffixes don't count for the letter: Ken Griffey Jr. → G. No suffix always means the Sr.: "Ken Griffey" is the dad — type Jr. for the son.
  • Close spellings count (Carl Yastremski, You Darvish), but if it's close to two different players, fix it. An exact name always wins: Scott Servais and Scott Service are two players.
  • Shared name, like Will Smith? You get 3 extra seconds to tap the position: Pitcher or Catcher. The other Will Smith is still up for grabs. Same name and same position (the two pitchers named Pedro Martinez) counts as one player.
  • Double letters (Sammy Sosa) reverse the order when 3+ are playing. With 2 players, a double letter means you go again, and you keep going until you name a player who isn't a double.
  • Strike for a repeat, a wrong letter, a non-big-leaguer, or running out the 15-second clock. Three strikes, you're out.
  • A strike is only pending until the next player seals it with a right answer. Miss too, and you've got a pending strike of your own.
  • If it goes all the way back around to whoever missed first, nobody gets a strike — that player starts fresh with any name not yet played.
  • To pause, you must submit your answer first. Nobody sees it until you unpause. A pause can last 8 hours — after that the game resets.

What are the results I'm actually supposed to deliver?

The means are not the end. The goal isn't to create a great weekend experience. The goal is to connect people to Jesus. Everything we do is about creating avenues for people to experience Him.

A lot of us have been trained to worry about the process instead of the outcome. So here's the filter I run things through.

### Five critical factors of ministry

1. **Are people coming to Jesus?** And how do you know?
2. **Are leaders being developed and multiplied?** What does our leadership pipeline look like? How are we empowering people and pushing decisions down? Are people replacing themselves?
3. **Are we getting new people in the circle?** This isn't about inviting. It's about training everyone to have conversations about Jesus and do life with other people. Do we actually feel like we're on a mission from Jesus?
4. **Are we growing giving people?** Teach, motivate and equip people around the value of giving, because giving is an expression of our love for Jesus. That happens through relationship, not a quick transition moment from the stage.
5. **Are we keeping people connected?** Through discipleship and serving.

### It's all about people

Don't make the process the end. We don't just want more volunteers. We want volunteers so that they can grow in their relationship with Christ.

If you want interns, don't start with "we need interns." Start with "how do we multiply leaders?" If interns fit into that answer, it'll happen naturally.

Start with the five questions above, and you'll find yourself coming up with more creative and intentional answers.

### Choose purpose over preference

The point of the church is to grow and multiply. Check your comfort level. Are you in a rut, doing the same old things the same old way?

And pay attention to who gives you energy and who drains it.

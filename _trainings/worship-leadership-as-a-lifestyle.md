A worship leader isn't only a great song singer. A worship leader leads worship with their life.

### Faithful in the small things

When you're on time, punctual and communicative, people see you as trustworthy, caring and competent. So I schedule and plan ahead. I let people know if I'm going to miss something. I take care of everything I'm responsible for. With my family, I'm present, and I have the tough conversations when they're needed.

### The Holy Spirit is working in you every day

On Sunday I'm able to do things I'm not good at because I lean on the Holy Spirit to work through me. I have to do the same thing every other day of the week.

Harold Best warned that musicians "can create the impression that God is more present when music is being made than when it is not." God has promised He's with us. The goal is to trust that promise, so we don't feel like we have to muster something up to make His presence happen.

What if Galatians 2:20, Romans 8:11 and Colossians 1:27 were actually true? We'd all say "of course it's true, it's Scripture." But what would it look like to live like the same Spirit that raised Jesus from the dead is the Spirit we have access to every day?

We look at David as a worship leader and songwriter whose lyrics still show up in our songs today. He was also an adulterer and a murderer, and a man after God's own heart. What do we have that David didn't? The Holy Spirit. If God could work through David, imagine what the Holy Spirit can do through us.

### Character

Character is the mental and moral qualities of a person. Romans 12 says that offering our bodies as a living sacrifice is true and proper worship. So a true worship leader doesn't build their life around their talent. They build it around a humble, sacrificial character that reflects the heart of God.

There's a line from *Extreme Ownership* I come back to a lot: there are no bad teams, only bad leaders. The leader owns everything in their world, including the mistakes, and makes a plan to win.

Here's the good news. If a worship leader is great at leading songs but misses a meeting or a deadline, life goes on. People will still find Jesus. The bad news is that when a lack of discipline creeps into daily life, when things aren't managed, loved ones don't get your time, and accountability isn't taken seriously, people stop trusting you, and that's when a leader starts teetering toward moral failure.

So lean on the Holy Spirit in every part of life. Find help. Reach out to a mentor who's more organized than you. Find systems that work and don't settle. Become a humble, reliable, trustworthy person in word and deed, one small step at a time.

### Fight burnout

Church staff burn out all the time. If you believe God called you where you are for a purpose, protect that:

- **Everything does not rely on you.** If you were gone tomorrow, someone else would step into your role.
- **Learn to say no** to extra tasks, requests from other ministries, and things outside of work you don't want to do.
- **Find community.** Friends, mentors and coaches who aren't connected to your job, people you can do life with.
- **Take your time off,** and use the slower seasons to rest.

Ezekiel 33:32 describes people who heard the prophet like "one who sings love songs with a beautiful voice," but never put his words into practice. If songs were going to save people, they would have done it by now. Our lives have to model what and who we believe in.

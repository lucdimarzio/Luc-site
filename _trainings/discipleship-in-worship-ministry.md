Right now, worship leaders are often chosen for their musical ability more than their character or spiritual maturity. Their job is to lead congregational singing, and that focus on talent can create a disconnect between what a leader can do and how deep they actually are.

> Character, not talent, governs the rise and fall of most leaders. (John C. Maxwell)

### Why worship matters

Worship magnifies God (Psalm 34:1). It nurtures our hearts (Psalm 100), realigns our priorities (Colossians 1:27), draws us close to Him (Psalm 73:28, James 4:8), brings joy (Psalm 97:1) and renews our strength (Isaiah 40:31).

### Equipping the saints

A worship pastor's main job is to grow and empower people to live out their God-given callings. That means spiritual growth, developing leaders, and building a servant-hearted team.

### Mentorship says "sit with me." Discipleship says "follow me."

1. Commit to Jesus and grow in intimacy with Him
2. Model Christ-like character through accountability
3. Equip others with the truth and serve alongside them
4. Commission them to make more disciples
5. Reproduce and expand God's Kingdom

### Discipleship through worship

Move beyond passing along information. Create an environment where people imitate what they see and are actually changed. A few questions to ask ourselves:

- Are we leading songs, or people?
- Are we mentoring, or discipling?
- Are we leading people to a place we've been?
- Are we equipping and empowering the people we lead?

### Songs are not enough

If songs were actually what changed people, they would have done it by now. There are more talented singers waiting tables across the country than there are in our churches. The things that matter are Christ-like character, spiritual growth, leadership and accountability, and a heart of service.

We like to think "we are the generation." Probably not. What if our job is to hand the baton to the next generation at the best clip we can? We can be like Jesus, who told His disciples they'd do even greater things than He did. Or we can be like Saul, who heard a song about David's ten thousands and let it ruin him.

### From sprint to relay

> Life is not a sprint, it's not a marathon, it's a relay. If you still think life is a marathon, you still think life is about you. And it doesn't matter how fast you run if you drop the baton. (Aaron Keyes)

We're not just here to inspire people. We're here to empower the ones who'll carry this after us, and to build a legacy through discipleship and relationship.

### What if...

What if worship leaders were people whose ability never outran their character? What if their calling was bigger than helping people sing better, because Sunday is too small a goal? And what if, because they'd been given room to grow and fail, they had wisdom to pass on to others?

> The Sovereign Lord has given me a well-instructed tongue, to know the word that sustains the weary. (Isaiah 50:4)

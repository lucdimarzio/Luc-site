Our mirror is the Word, and the Word is Jesus. If you don't see yourself in that mirror, you're not living in God's truth about you. We're all being transformed, we all have flaws, and God is finishing His work in us.

### Who are you?

Our past wins and failures shape how we see ourselves. So do our emotions, like joy, sadness and insecurity (James 1:24-25). But God's Word shows us who we really are: beloved children of God (Galatians 3:26, Galatians 4:6-7, John 1:12, Romans 8:14-16). Through the lens of Scripture, we find our real worth and purpose in Christ.

### The gospel truth

Because of Jesus' sacrifice on the cross, our sins are forgiven and we're seen as righteous before God, because His righteousness is credited to us.

> For our sake He made Him to be sin who knew no sin, so that in Him we might become the righteousness of God. (2 Corinthians 5:21)

Abram believed God, and it was counted to him as righteousness (Genesis 15:6). What if we really believed God says we're righteous? What would it look like to live like it?

### I am a new creation

The old has gone (2 Corinthians 5:17). We're being renewed day by day (2 Corinthians 3:18), growing into Christ's likeness, living for His purpose, and free from sin's power and guilt (Romans 8:2). And that new identity is forever.

### I have authority

- **Power to overcome darkness.** We aren't victims, we're victors (1 John 5:4, Romans 8:37).
- **Dominion over the enemy.** We've been given authority over the works of the enemy (Luke 10:19).
- **Ambassadors of the Kingdom.** We represent God's Kingdom on earth and bring light, hope and healing (2 Corinthians 5:18-20).

### I am seated with Christ

We're seated with Christ in heavenly places (Ephesians 2:6). That's a position of authority and victory, with confident access to God's presence (Hebrews 4:14-16).

### I am adopted and accepted

We're not servants anymore, we're friends of God (John 15:15). We've been adopted into His family as co-heirs with Christ (Galatians 4:7, Romans 8:17). We're not strangers or outsiders, we're members of God's household (Ephesians 2:19), secure, loved and valued as His own.

### We are heirs of God

We're adopted through Christ's sacrifice, invited to embrace our identity as God's children, share in the family inheritance, walk in the Spirit as co-heirs with Christ, and reflect the glory of our Father (Matthew 5:16).

### The Church

Scripture calls the Church the Bride of Christ (Revelation 19:7, Ephesians 5). Chosen, made holy, and loved by a Savior who gave Himself for her.

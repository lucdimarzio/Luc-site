These are the maxims I want every person on my team to carry. Everything else we train on comes back to these.

### I am a leader of people, not songs

Anybody can lead a song. Few people can lead people. So we're called to something bigger than just leading songs.

### I am making disciples of Jesus, not making people feel intensely about Him

The goal isn't for the room to sing louder. Songs don't change lives, Jesus does. If songs were going to change lives, they would have done it by now. What we're after has Kingdom-sized implications.

### Sunday is too small to live for

Sunday is the fruit of how we live Monday through Saturday. I don't look to Sunday to fill something inside of me. I look forward to it because I get to be with the rest of the body of Christ and remember His goodness and faithfulness together.

### God is not impressed by me, but He is pleased when I give Him my heart

God isn't impressed by the cool things I can do. He's pleased when I give Him my heart.

### God does not need things from me, but He has something for me

God isn't a praise-mongering God. He's already complete. He's good, and He has good things for me, so I come expecting them and I lean in.

### I am not alone. I am not what I create.

I don't carry this by myself. And my worth isn't defined by what I make.

### The challenge

Live like I believe what I sing about on stage. Be the same person in every room. If the Holy Spirit is at work in me in every moment, then how people encounter me is also how they encounter Christ.

Part of inviting people into the presence of God assumes you're actually at the party you're inviting them to. We can never lead someone to a place we've never been. Nobody on the team is without sin. We're all broken and in need of God, so we commit together to the process of growth. And whether you think so or not, being on a stage makes you a leader in the eyes of the church.

Integrity in your public and private life is what makes worship authentic. Being a living sacrifice means actually living out the words we sing. That's why character and excellence go together for us, held with humility. If you're on this team, you have a God-given gift for music, and it's worth stewarding well. We work hard at getting better musically while we keep growing as followers of Jesus.

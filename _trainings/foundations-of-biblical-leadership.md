Bad leadership is pastoral failure. It usually comes from not living out the humility, service and compassion at the center of the gospel. When leaders put their own agenda ahead of the people they lead, they miss the pastoral calling.

### The model is the gospel

Jesus is the ultimate model for leadership and life. We follow His humility and servanthood, lead with His love and compassion, and draw wisdom from His teaching in the Gospels. When we follow His model and see life through His lens, He changes lives.

> And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age. (Matthew 28:20)

### What gospel-centered leadership looks like

- **Humility.** Knowing your limits and seeking wisdom from others.
- **Integrity.** Your words and your actions line up.
- **Grace.** Extending the same undeserved favor Christ extends to us.
- **Courage.** Standing firm in the face of opposition, the way Jesus did.
- **Service.** Putting other people's needs first, like Jesus washing His disciples' feet.

### The spiritual battle

Leadership is spiritual warfare. Put on the full armor of God (Ephesians 6:11), stay alert (Matthew 26:41), pray without ceasing (1 Thessalonians 5:16-18), and persevere (Romans 5:1-5), knowing God holds you up (Isaiah 41:10-13).

### The heart of a servant

Servanthood is the path of humility (Mark 10:45). Sacrifice is the essence of love (Romans 5:6). Leadership comes through service (Matthew 20:25-28). A servant's heart, rooted in humility, sacrifice and selfless love, is the foundation of real Christian leadership.

> In confession lies the defiance of pride. (C.S. Lewis)

### Restoration and longevity

- **Stumbling.** When leaders make mistakes, they need grace and restoration.
- **Perspective.** Look at challenges through Christ's lens.
- **Legacy.** Build a heritage of faith and integrity instead of chasing short wins.
- **Perseverance.** Long-term impact takes patience and endurance.
- **Fruit.** Real success is measured by lasting spiritual fruit.

### The presence of God

True leadership is born out of humility and reverence toward Jesus, and the understanding that all authority comes from God's grace. As leaders, we're stewards, and that takes faith and a constant awareness of His presence.

About

Hey, I'm Luc.

Worship pastor, music nerd, CHH head since I was five, and a big fan of people.

Growing up I was kind of two kids at once. I was a classically trained singer and pianist, and I was also the kid who'd rather be at the ballpark than pretty much anywhere else. Hip-hop was there the whole time too. I was five when I first heard Stephen Wiley's Bible Break, and I've been hooked on Christian hip-hop ever since. That was {{ hiphop_years }} years ago.

I went to college for music education and hit a pretty low point while I was there. My buddy Chad kept inviting me to church, and that's where I met Jesus. After that I went to Bible college and spent three years on the road with my band, Frontline. Those guys are still lifelong friends. We binged way too much 24, I got to watch my friend Bill see the ocean for the first time, and Joel met his wife along the way. Funny thing is, it wasn't until after those tours that it all took root and I fully surrendered. I was baptized in November 2004.

I've been in full-time ministry since 2006, leading worship and pastoring creative people at churches in Colorado and now Las Vegas. At the end of the day I just care about people, and creativity and art are the best way I know to point people to Jesus.

I've always got my ear to the streets. I love finding the artists nobody's talking about yet. I wrote about 350 on NewH2O way back, before he was one of the biggest names in Christian rap. Since 2017 I've been writing about CHH for NewH2O, Rapzilla and Midnight Donuts. Mostly that's Bar Exams, which are my notes from the first listen of a new project, plus Luc's Picks and interviews with artists like Trip Lee, Nic D, FLAME, Braille, and Selah the Corner.

I make music too. Masterpeace is my shot at mixing hip-hop, spoken word and church music together.

When I'm not doing all that, I'm probably watching the Cubs with my wife, in the pool with my kids, or picking up another fitted. Any team works, as long as it's not the Brewers or the Cardinals.

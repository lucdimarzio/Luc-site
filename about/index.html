About

Hey, I'm Luc.

Worship pastor, music nerd, CHH head since I was five, and a big fan of people.

Growing up, I was kind of two kids at once (which is funny, because now I'm a dad to twins). I was a classically trained singer, and I was also the kid who'd rather be at the ballpark than pretty much anywhere else. Hip-hop was there for me the whole time, too. I was five years old when I first heard Stephen Wiley's song “Bible Break,” and I've been hooked on Christian hip-hop ever since. That was {{ hiphop_years }} years ago.

I went to college for music education and hit a pretty low point while I was there. My buddy Chad kept inviting me to church, and that's where I met Jesus. After that, I decided to try Ozark Christian College, where I spent three years studying and traveling with my band. Those people are still lifelong friends. I'll always cherish binging way too much 24, watching Bill see the ocean for the first time, and being there when Joel met his wife. It actually wasn't until after those tours that my convictions and my calling took root and I fully surrendered. I was baptized in November 2004.

I've been in full-time ministry since 2006, leading worship and pastoring creative people at churches in Colorado and now Nevada. At the end of the day, I just care about people, and creativity and art are the best ways I know to point people to Jesus.

You know… I've always got my ear to the streets. I love finding the artists nobody's talking about yet. I was rocking with artists like Alcott, 350, and Alano Adan before you were. Since 2017, I've been writing about CHH for outlets like NewH2O, Rapzilla, and Midnight Donuts. Mostly that's been Bar Exams, which are my notes from my first listen of a new project. There's also Luc's Picks, which spotlight artists you haven't heard of yet, and interviews with artists like Trip Lee, Nic D, FLAME, Braille, and Selah the Corner.

I make music too. Masterpeace is my shot at mixing hip-hop, spoken word, and church music. It's a few years old now, but I'm still proud of the work I put into that project.

When I'm not doing all that, I'm probably watching the Cubs or binging TV with my wife, in the pool with my kids, or picking up another fitted. Any team works, as long as it's not the Brewers or the Cardinals.

Plays like a mixtape. Too many skits. Beats are great, above the bar. Main vocals are in the front of the mix, makes for the listening experience to be a bit uncomfortable.

Fluidity of the album doesn’t work well. Even though I’m reminded a bit of Busta Rhymes’ *E.L.E.*, having a skit before each track sacrifices listenability and causes me to want to skip tracks more often than I would if it was just songs.

Hooks are also above the bar. Lyrics though are at or below the bar. Schemes are easy, and when paired with corny concepts I wasn’t feeling them. I slumped hard from tracks 8–20.

Overall a below the bar album, disappointing release from an artist we’d been waiting on for years.
